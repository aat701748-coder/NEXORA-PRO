About NEXORA SND

NEXORA SND is a Smart Network Diagnostic platform designed to present network measurements clearly and help interpret service behavior.

Cloud Edition limitation
This edition measures HTTP request timing from Netlify's serverless environment to configured public endpoints. It is not an ICMP ping tool, does not measure packet loss, and cannot diagnose your home LAN without a Raspberry Pi or another local measurement agent.
